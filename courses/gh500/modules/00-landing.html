How the GH-500 exam weights its domains

Spend study time in proportion to exam weight. Heavy domains carry the most points. This course covers all five domains — 100% of the exam.

Domain 1: GHAS Features & Functionality (this course) 15%
Domain 2: Configure & use secret scanning 15%
Domain 3: Configure & use Dependabot and Dependency Review 35%
Domain 4: Configure & use Code Scanning with CodeQL 25%
Domain 5: Best practices & corrective measures 10%

Source: Official GH-500 study guide (Microsoft Learn). Note: the exam has been undergoing restructuring — newer materials may show 6 domains with Supply Chain Security and Code Security as product names. The fundamentals taught here are stable across both structures.
